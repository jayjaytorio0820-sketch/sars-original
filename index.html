Puto made for sharing

Sars is a modern Filipino comfort pastry kitchen. Our signature is the small-bite puto cake: soft, a little cheesy, and sized so everyone at the table gets one more.

We think the best part of merienda isn't the food. It's the moment around it.

  • Morning moments. A quiet coffee and a few bites before the day starts.
  • Sharing. A bilao in the middle, hands reaching in from every side.
  • Small wins. A breaktime treat for finishing the hard part.
